# Strategic review checklist

- Verify remote exact PR/head/order/report/implementation parent and report-only path.
- Review all objective rounds, actual diff, numbered acceptance, negative evidence,
  tested SHAs and failed/skipped/pending/missing checks; exercise named boundaries.
- Check scope, architecture, documentation, dependencies/rights/secrets and protected
  Qwen/resources. Verify exact CRITICAL append and prior history; assess aggregate risk.
- State strongest reason not to merge and why continued development is acceptable.
- Require all named checks completed success at reviewed head; changed head re-review.
- Verify approved merge method and development-only side effects; obey protections.
- After merge verify PR and remote default head; preserve SELF evidence across squash.
- Development merge grants no release; applicable DHA plus separate human authority
  and fresh ICA remain required at target gates. No self-certified milestone.
