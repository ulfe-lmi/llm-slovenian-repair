# Project decision policy

- **D0 — ordinary engineering choice.** Choose, test and document an ordinary
  reversible module layout, queue implementation or scoped dependency after
  compatible rights. Failed tests, missing corpus/auth, weak statistical
  support and ordinary bugs are evidence work, not judgment debt.
- **D1 — consequential contained option.** Strategy must investigate and
  provisionally choose, compare credible alternatives, assess
  staging, reversibility and blast radius, mitigate and test, and state the
  strongest counterargument. All five conditions apply: governing
  law or evidence unresolved; materially different investigated alternatives
  requiring choice; material security, privacy, authentication, authorization, data, trust, exposure or
  release consequences; a safe testable provisional path without D2 crossing; and a
  plausible competent human rejection or change before the exact gate. Excluded:
  TODOs or style, ordinary dependencies, speculation, low-impact or repeated
  dilemmas. Keep uncertainty explicit; no routine human interruption simply
  because a reversible choice feels uncomfortable.
- **D2 — human-controlled.** Real sensitive data under unapproved trust,
  production or deploying merge, irreversible data, credential changes, real
  exposure, network or security posture, protected Qwen changes, legal
  obligations, or redefinition of human intent. Analyze and recommend, then
  stop real action; safe isolated preparation remains separately scoped.
  Unknown rights need investigation or a permitted alternative, not a
  CRIT-derived license. Lowering strict acceptance past independent evidence
  changes the product agreement.

Strategy authors the exact canonical entry and ID. Coding reports candidates or
appends ordered bytes before the implementation SHA. The five-condition attestation is
declarative; mitigation and supersession remain pending. Human dispositions are
exactly ACCEPTED, REJECTED, CHANGE REQUIRED or DEFERRED; only the latest
attributable verified ACCEPTED clears a registered gate. A name in Markdown
does not authenticate. Review aggregate risk at gates; none of this grants
independently missing deployment authority.
